How to Play

  • A / Left Arrow: Move left
  • D / Right Arrow: Move right
  • W / Up Arrow: Jump
  • Space: Throw a salsa bottle

Collect coins and salsa bottles.

Jump on chickens or hit enemies with bottles.

Defeat the endboss to win the level.
